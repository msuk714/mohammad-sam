Khanabadosh Glamps

A build-and-SEO engagement where search strategy shaped the website structure, content and destination relevance before launch.

Websitekhanabadosh.pk
EngagementMar 2025 - Jun 2025
RoleWebsite Development + SEO Strategy & Implementation
SectorHospitality

Executive Overview

SEO was integrated into the site structure and content from launch rather than added afterward. Destination-led targeting around Murree and Kumrat produced page-one visibility beyond branded searches.

Strategy & Execution

  • Developed the website with SEO considerations integrated into site structure and content from the beginning.
  • Aligned search strategy with the way travellers search for glamping and resort options in Murree and Kumrat.
  • Structured and optimized priority pages around glamping, resort, pod and destination themes.
  • Built architecture connecting the brand, accommodation offering and destination relevance.
