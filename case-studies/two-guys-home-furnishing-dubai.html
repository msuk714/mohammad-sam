Two Guys Home Furnishing Dubai

An organic-growth engagement focused on increasing discoverability across high-intent furnishing, flooring, blinds and curtain searches in the UAE market.

Websitewww.twoguys.ae
EngagementJun 2024 - Apr 2025
RoleSEO Strategy & Execution
SectorHome Furnishing

Executive Overview

The project expanded Two Guys across home-furnishing search demand in Dubai. The tracked period recorded a major increase in impressions and a near-doubling of clicks, while average position improved slightly.

Strategy & Execution

  • Expanded search visibility across commercially relevant furnishing, flooring, blinds and curtain themes.
  • Improved traffic acquisition as a broader search footprint translated into more organic visits.
  • Evaluated CTR and average position in the context of much wider query coverage.
