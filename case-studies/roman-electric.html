Roman Electric

A four-month SEO growth engagement designed to increase qualified search visibility beyond existing brand demand while improving the technical and structural foundations of high-intent service pages.

Websiteromanelectric.com
EngagementDec 2025 - Apr 2026
RoleSenior SEO Strategist / Strategy Lead
SectorHome Services

Executive Overview

The engagement combined technical refinement, service-silo architecture and local intent alignment to expand Roman Electric beyond existing branded demand. Impressions more than doubled while clicks also increased, creating a much larger search footprint to mature over time.

Strategy & Execution

  • Reorganized the service architecture into clear Electrical, Plumbing and HVAC silos to strengthen topical relevance.
  • Addressed Core Web Vitals and mobile-usability issues across priority service pages.
  • Implemented local-business schema, refined page targeting and aligned service content with local and near-me intent.
  • Expanded visibility beyond existing branded demand into a broader set of service and discovery queries.
