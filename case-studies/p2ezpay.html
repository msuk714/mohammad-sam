P2EzPay

A focused organic-growth engagement that moved the site from limited search traction toward a broader, more commercially relevant query footprint.

Websitep2ezpay.com
EngagementDec 2025 - Apr 2026
RoleSenior SEO Strategist / Strategy Lead
SectorFinTech

Executive Overview

The work addressed technical foundations, clarified commercial intent and organized content around payment security and global transactions. From a small starting base, impressions, clicks and average position all moved in the right direction.

Strategy & Execution

  • Audited crawl efficiency, schema-markup errors and foundational issues that could limit search-engine understanding.
  • Reworked core landing pages, H1-H3 structures and metadata around high-intent commercial themes.
  • Built a cluster-led content framework around Payment Security, Global Transactions and supporting topics.
  • Expanded the searchable footprint while clicks, impressions and average position improved together.
