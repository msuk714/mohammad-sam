Blinds and Curtains Dubai

A migration-critical SEO engagement where technical continuity, URL preservation and post-launch monitoring were central to protecting existing organic value.

Websiteblindsandcurtains.ae
EngagementJun 2024 - Apr 2025
RoleSEO Migration Strategy & Execution
SectorHome Interiors
MilestoneWordPress to custom platform migration in Jan 2025

Executive Overview

This engagement treated migration SEO as a continuity problem: redirects, URL preservation, crawlability, category structure and post-launch monitoring were planned together. The recorded Search Console data shows substantial visibility retained through the platform change.

Strategy & Execution

  • Managed the SEO transition with detailed 301 redirect mapping and URL-continuity planning.
  • Monitored crawlability, indexation and ranking behaviour after launch so migration issues could be identified quickly.
  • Reorganized product and service hierarchy while protecting high-intent categories with existing search value.
  • Prioritized speed, Core Web Vitals and UX for image-heavy product galleries.
