My strategic approach

Find the growth constraint. Prioritize the right work. Prove the outcome.

01

Diagnose before optimizing

I start with technical health, search demand, query coverage, site structure and commercial priorities so the roadmap addresses the real constraint.

02

Build around user and business intent

Pages, internal links, content clusters and local or e-commerce structures are aligned with how qualified users search and how the business creates value.

03

Measure in context, not in isolation

Clicks, impressions, rankings, CTR, indexation and migration stability are interpreted together so decisions reflect the whole search picture.
